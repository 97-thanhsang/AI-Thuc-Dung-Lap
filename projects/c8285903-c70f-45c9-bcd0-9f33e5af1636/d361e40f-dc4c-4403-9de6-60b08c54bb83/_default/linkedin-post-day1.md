# LinkedIn Post - Day 1

## Post Draft

---

**I learned Angular before there was a "Senior" title for it.**

7 years ago, I started coding from my bedroom. No CS degree. No bootcamp. No mentor.

Just a laptop, free tutorials, and stubbornness.

Today? I'm a Senior Dev working with Angular and .NET daily.

Here's what nobody told me:

1. **"Self-taught" is not a limitation. It's an advantage.**
   I learned how to learn. That's a skill most CS grads never develop.

2. **The first year is the hardest.**
   I wrote code that makes me cringe today. But I wrote code. Every. Single. Day.

3. **Degrees don't make developers. Projects do.**
   My first "real" job came from a portfolio of side projects, not my resume.

If you're where I was 7 years ago - uncertain, self-doubting, wondering if you "belong" in tech:

You belong.

The only thing that separates senior devs from junior devs is time + practice. That's it.

What was your "day 1" moment in tech? 👇
