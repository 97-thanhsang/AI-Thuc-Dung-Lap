## Channel Strategy

### LinkedIn

- **Frequency:** 4 posts/week (Mon, Wed, Thu, Sun)
- **Format:** Long-form text posts, carousels, article links
- **Tone:** Professional, analytical, thought leadership
- **Goal:** Build authority, attract B2B audience

### Threads

- **Frequency:** 5 posts/week (Wed, Thu, Sat, Sun + daily replies)
- **Format:** Short text threads, quick tips, engagement
- **Tone:** Conversational, authentic, slightly casual
- **Goal:** Community building, viral reach

### TikTok

- **Frequency:** 4 videos/week (Tue, Thu, Fri, Sat)
- **Format:** 30-60sec vertical videos, screen recordings
- **Tone:** Energetic, visual, hook-driven
- **Goal:** Mass reach, younger audience, brand awareness

### YouTube

- **Frequency:** 3 videos/week (Mon, Tue/Fri alternating, 1 long-form)
- **Format:** 5-12min tutorials, reviews, deep-dives
- **Tone:** Educational, structured, value-first
- **Goal:** Evergreen content, SEO, affiliate revenue

### Facebook

- **Frequency:** 5 posts/week (Mon, Tue, Wed, Fri, Sun)
- **Format:** Video posts, image posts, group engagement
- **Tone:** Friendly, community-oriented, Vietnamese-first
- **Goal:** Local audience, community building, sharing

## Hiring Plan

### Phase 1: Immediate (Week 1-2) — CMO ✅ Submitted for Approval

**CMO — Chief Marketing Officer**

- **Role:** Own content strategy, channel management, brand voice
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Execute content calendar
  - Manage all 5 channels
  - Maintain brand voice consistency
  - Report weekly metrics to CEO
- **Reports to:** CEO

### Phase 2: Month 1 (Week 2-4) — Core Team

**Market Researcher**

- **Role:** SaaS & AI trend research
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Weekly AI tool landscape scan
  - Competitor content analysis
  - Affiliate program discovery & evaluation
  - Trend reports for content team
- **Reports to:** CMO

**Omnichannel Writer**

- **Role:** Content creation across all platforms
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Write LinkedIn posts, Threads, Facebook posts
  - Script YouTube videos and TikToks
  - Adapt one piece of content into 5 formats
  - Maintain Vietnamese natural tone
- **Reports to:** CMO

### Phase 3: Month 2 — Growth & Quality

**QA & Affiliate Manager**

- **Role:** Quality control + affiliate revenue optimization
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Review all content before publishing
  - Manage affiliate link placement and tracking
  - A/B test content formats
  - Revenue reporting and optimization
- **Reports to:** CMO
